About Me

Hi, I’m Emily. I’m a classically trained French horn player, a homeschool mother of four, and a lifelong learner who loves music, scripture, and her family. After being homeschooled myself through high school, I went on to earn a Bachelor of Music in French Horn Performance from the Hartt School of Music and a Master of Music in Contemporary Music Performance from Boston Conservatory at Berklee. Today, my days are filled with teaching our children at home, making music with our church arts community, and walking through scripture with the women in my life.
